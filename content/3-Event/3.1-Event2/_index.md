## Summary Report

### Event Objectives

The session aimed to provide a comprehensive understanding of different hosting options, dive deep into architecture design, and share practical guidance on how to choose the right hosting solution. It also covered best practices and real-world case studies to help participants make better technical decisions.

### Key Topics

**01. The Hosting Spectrum**
- Overview of various hosting models
- From traditional servers to modern cloud and serverless options
- Pros and cons of each approach

**02. Architecture Deep-Dive**
- Detailed examination of hosting architectures
- Key components and design considerations
- How architecture choices impact scalability, performance, and cost

**03. Choosing the Right Option**
- Criteria for selecting the appropriate hosting solution
- Factors to consider: workload type, budget, team skills, growth plans
- Decision-making framework

**04. Best Practices & Real-World Cases**
- Proven best practices in hosting and architecture
- Real-world case studies and lessons learned
- Common pitfalls and how to avoid them

**05. Conclusion & Discussion**
- Summary of key insights
- Open discussion and Q&A

### Key Takeaways

- There is no one-size-fits-all hosting solution; the best choice depends on specific requirements.
- Understanding the full hosting spectrum helps in making informed architecture decisions.
- Real-world cases provide valuable lessons that theory alone cannot offer.
- Architecture decisions should balance performance, cost, scalability, and operational complexity.

### Event Experience

Attending the session with speaker Quang Pham was insightful. The content progressed logically from high-level concepts (Hosting Spectrum) to deeper technical discussions and practical decision-making frameworks. The real-world cases helped connect theory with actual implementation challenges.

### Lessons Learned

- Always evaluate hosting options based on the specific context of the project.
- Architecture deep-dives are essential before making long-term technology choices.
- Learning from real-world cases helps avoid common mistakes.
- Continuous discussion and knowledge sharing improve decision quality.
